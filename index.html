Bake & Bloom

Line up three treats or sprouts to tidy up

How to play
  • Swap two neighboring pieces by tapping one, then the other, or by swiping. A swap only counts if it lines up 3 or more.
  • Each level gives you a set number of moves and a goal: reach a score, scrape off all the frosting, or fill an order.
  • 4 in a row bakes a Striped piece that clears its whole row or column.
  • An L or T shape wraps a Gift Box that bursts 3×3, falls, and bursts again.
  • 4 in a square frees a Butterfly that flies to a piece you need (or frosting) and clears it.
  • 5 in a row makes a Sprinkle Bomb. Swap it with any piece to clear every piece of that kind.
  • Swap two specials together for a bigger combo.
  • Run out of moves and you lose a heart. Hearts grow back, one every 30 minutes.
